Product Decision & Experiment Canvas

Frame a problem and choose a test.

State the decision

What decision must be made, by whom, and by when?

Describe the problem

Name the audience, situation and difficulty. Separate observations from assumptions.

Write a testable assumption

We believe [change] helps [audience] achieve [outcome]. What evidence would contradict this?

Design the smallest test

Choose a prototype or conversation, participants, task, observation method and limitations.

Set a decision rule

Before testing, define proceed, revise and stop signals. Record results and the next owner.

Worked example

Illustrative example: a founder tests whether first-time users understand an onboarding task using a paper prototype. Observed confusion informs the next iteration; this is not proof of market demand.
