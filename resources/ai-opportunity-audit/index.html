AI Opportunity & Workflow Audit

Identify one workflow worth improving.

Choose a workflow

Describe the trigger, owner, inputs, steps and expected output. Avoid choosing a whole department.

Establish a baseline

Record frequency, typical minutes per run, rework and the evidence source. Mark estimates.

Check suitability

Is the task repeatable? Are inputs accessible? Can a person check quality? Identify sensitive data and required permissions.

Choose a small experiment

Specify one step AI will assist, a human review point, a quality threshold and a stop condition.

Decide what happens next

Compare the trial against baseline. Keep, revise or stop; assign an owner and review date.

Worked example

Illustrative example: a team tests drafting internal meeting summaries from approved notes. A person checks every action and owner before sharing. No time savings are assumed.
