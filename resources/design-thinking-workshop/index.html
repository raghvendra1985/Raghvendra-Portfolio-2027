Work through the activity

Five steps, in order. .

  1. 01

    Step 1: Observe before interpreting

    Choose a real context. Record three things someone does or says, then separate each observation from your interpretation. Ask permission before recording people.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Frame the need

    Write: [person] needs a way to [job] because [observed constraint]. Add what you still do not know. Avoid embedding a preferred solution.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Generate alternatives

    Write three meaningfully different responses. Change the interaction, service arrangement or environment—not only the visual style.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Make a small prototype

    Pick the riskiest assumption. Build a paper, role-play or click-through prototype that can expose it in one task.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Test and reflect

    Describe the task without explaining the solution. Record what the participant did, where they hesitated and what you will change. A small test is learning, not proof of market demand.

Worked example

Illustrative workshop example: a student observes confusion at a library returns desk. They test a paper sign and a revised service handoff before proposing an app.
