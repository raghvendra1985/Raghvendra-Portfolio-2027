Product Strategy &

Make complex things easier to use.

AI Automation &
Workflow Design

For teams whose repeat work has outgrown disconnected tools and manual handoffs.

Make work flow—with people in control.

Design connected, AI-assisted processes around the work your team actually does.

A few practical
questions.

Do we need a defined AI project?

No. We can begin by understanding your goals and current workflows, then identify whether AI is useful and where to start.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. We can shape the engagement around your product, design, and technical teams, with responsibilities agreed at the outset.

How long does an engagement take?

That depends on the problem, access to people and systems, and the depth of implementation. We agree milestones and timelines in the proposal.

How do pricing and scope work?

We first discuss the challenge and intended outcome, then propose the scope, deliverables, fees, and responsibilities for review.
