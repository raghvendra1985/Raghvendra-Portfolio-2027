Stop Designing Screens. Start Designing Decisions.

Most design challenges inside growing companies are rarely screen problems. They are decision problems wearing a visual disguise.

When four product squads are shipping features in parallel, alignment breaks down not because designers lack craft, but because no one agreed on the boundary conditions: who owns what definition of done, what trade-offs are accepted, and how uncertainty is documented.

The Three-Input Verification Gate
Before handing any AI prompt or automated workflow to your engineering squad, run three deliberately diverse, non-sensitive test inputs. Log every hallucination, revise constraints, and establish human review criteria before code is committed.
