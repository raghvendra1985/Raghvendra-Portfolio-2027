Design Useful Products. Stop Designing Screens.

Most design bottlenecks in scaling technology organizations are not aesthetic defects—they are unaddressed decision problems masquerading as interface revisions.

When multiple product squads ship concurrently, consistency collapses because boundary conditions remain undefined: what tradeoffs are sanctioned, who validates the outputs, and where uncertainty must be retained rather than masked.

Core Practice · Try This Week
The 3-Input Verification Checkpoint
Before deploying any automated workflow or generative prompt, benchmark three deliberately diverse, non-sensitive inputs. Document hallucinations, refine operational constraints, and establish clear human verification gates before code is frozen.

During my tenure scaling the design organization at NYE Money across 10M+ users, we eliminated circular revisions by standardizing disclosures and reusable evaluation criteria across web, iOS, and Android squads.
