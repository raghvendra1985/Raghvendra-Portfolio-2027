A design team does not need more meetings. It needs a critique system.

More review time does not create better work. Clear standards, visible decisions, and useful feedback do.

By Raghvendra Singh · Original personal-site article. Read the original ↗

A critique system makes quality visible before the review starts.

Meetings are not a craft system

When quality slips, the default response is another review. The calendar fills. The work does not get clearer. Critique without a standard is just opinion with a meeting link.

What a system actually contains

A written quality bar. A decision log. A named owner. A format that separates taste from risk. Teams that have these four things spend less time arguing and more time shipping.

Make the standard usable

If juniors cannot apply the bar without a principal in the room, it is not a system. It is a bottleneck wearing a framework’s clothing.

Honest note

I am describing the shape of the system, not claiming a team transformed overnight. The test is whether the bar works when you are not in the room.

Framework

Critique System

Quality visible before the review starts. Opinion with a meeting link is not a craft system.

Quality bar

A written standard juniors can apply without a principal in the room.

Decision log

What was chosen, what was cut, and why.

Named owner

Someone accountable for the call, not the calendar.

Taste vs risk

A format that separates preference from harm.

Takeaway

Write the quality bar so a junior can apply it. If they cannot, you do not have a system.
