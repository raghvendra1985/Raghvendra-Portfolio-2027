# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary.** Recruiters and hiring leaders evaluating senior product-design leadership. They arrive under time pressure, scan for contribution and organisational weight, then verify claims against published case studies, résumé, or Contact.

**Secondary.** Founders seeking product leadership, AI-product advisory, or systems help. They need to see founder-built products and complex-platform work without mistaking the site for a services brochure.

**Tertiary.** Design peers, students, and collaborators exploring the thinking, writing, and tools. They browse System, Notes, Products, and Ask; conversion is optional.

**Primary job.** Understand the contribution quickly, verify it through published evidence, and continue confidently to a case study, résumé, or Contact.

## Product Purpose

This is the personal brand site for Raghvendra Singh — Product Design Leader, Systems Thinker, and AI Product Builder. It must feel authored, culturally rooted, restrained, and systems-led: a premium editorial portfolio that behaves like a modern digital product, not a SaaS shell.

Success looks like:

1. One clear present-day positioning on first contact.
2. Differentiated proof (enterprise leadership, founder products, AI building, systems work) that matches what case studies and Ask return.
3. Intentional paths into Work, About, résumé, and Contact — without reopening surfaces that already cohere.

## Positioning

Authored systems practice made visible: contribution before catalogue, published evidence over capability claims, and a methodology page (System) that connects how decisions are made to Work’s contribution groups — Product direction, Complex systems, AI and founder products, and Enterprise leadership.

## Operating Context

Recruiters and founders scan under time pressure on web (desktop and stacked mobile). Primary paths: Homepage → Work / About / System / Notes / Contact; Ask answers from published sources only. Charm corridor and hanging charms are part of authored character on desktop, not a second product. Shared navigation, footer, and Ask chrome stay consistent across surfaces.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Published Work Index V2 with Featured, contribution groups, Archive, continuation, and Work analytics.
- System methodology modules (Focus, Method to evidence, Products as practice, Decision Log, Experiments, Writing and field notes, Roadmap, Archive).
- About career narrative; Notes/Knowledge articles; Contact delivery; Ask retrieval grounded in site sources.
- Do not invent testimonials, metrics, or unpublished case results.
- Do not reopen frozen surfaces except for sustained production failure.

**Frozen reference material (do not edit unless sustained production failure is confirmed):** Homepage (legacy “Knowledge” label on the home chapter may remain), About, Contact, Work Index V2 (`/work`), featured case-study interiors, System (`/system` — structure, methodology copy, contribution map, Ask System sources), Notes (`/knowledge` — Notes naming, format shelves, featured curation, article rails, Ask Notes labels; URLs stay `/knowledge`), shared navigation, footer, Ask UI chrome, charm system, and visual tokens (`styles/tokens.css` identity).

**Next open surface:** Studio — reveal the life and environment around the work without becoming another About page or portfolio index.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: calm, specific, evidence-first. Personality: authored, restrained, systems-led.
- Cultural rootedness via charm corridor and working symbols is character, not bolted-on decoration.
- Visual identity locked in DESIGN.md / tokens: navy, mist, green, gold; Bricolage Grotesque + Manrope; square chrome; no cream-as-personality, purple-glow kits, or dashboard shells.

## Evidence on Hand

- Case studies and Work index data: `case-studies/`, `/work`, `/work/[slug]`
- System / founder OS: `founder-os/`, `/system`
- About: `about/`, `/about`
- Notes: `knowledge/`, `/knowledge`
- Contact: `/contact` + contact API
- Ask: `concierge/`
- Résumé download and Contact intents as published on site

Do not fabricate awards, client quotes, or performance claims beyond published case study copy.

## Product Principles

1. **Contribution before catalogue.** Lead with what was decided and shipped, not with a flat inventory of clients.
2. **Evidence chain.** Claims must match case studies, Ask answers, and homepage proof where those surfaces already publish them.
3. **One appearance per project.** A study may sit in Featured or in one contribution group — never both.
4. **Authored restraint.** Improve hierarchy and finish without inventing a second brand.
5. **Scan on mobile first.** Stacked reading with role, year, contribution, and evidence type visible without hover.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor. Keyboard navigation, visible focus (gold), semantic HTML, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for motion from `animations/`. Body and large text contrast against mist / navy must remain readable; muted ink is navy mixed toward mist, not washed gray. Lighthouse Accessibility target: 100.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI portfolio templates and “premium SaaS” landing kits (purple-on-white, glow stacks, pill clusters, hero-metric strips).
- Cream / sand / parchment body as the whole personality.
- Dashboard or product-shell navigation for a personal brand surface.
- Audience-filter overhead that makes recruiters sort instead of scan.
- Chronological “next project” that ignores contribution relationships.
- Duplicating the same project across index groups.
- Hover-only facts that fail stacked mobile scanning.
- Rewriting frozen Homepage, About, Contact, Work, System, featured interiors, Ask, charms, or tokens to chase a new aesthetic lane.
