How it works

Your AI twin, live
in 60 seconds.

Not a template filler. Not generic ChatGPT output. A system that reads you, learns from you, and sounds like you — then gets sharper every time you write.

01 🧬
Build your AI Clone
Paste your LinkedIn URL and website. In 60 seconds, the system reads your posts, bio, and writing across the web and builds a 3,000-word persona document — your tone, beliefs, vocabulary, what you never say, and how you open stories. This is the DNA of every post.
02 ✍️
Write, dictate & refine
Type a prompt, speak via voice dictation, or pick from trending ideas. Claude generates in your voice. Every edit, every slash command, every "remember this" is captured as memory and injected into the next generation. The AI gets sharper with every session.
03 📡
Schedule & close the loop
Preview in a live LinkedIn mockup. Publish via official LinkedIn API to personal profile or company page. Analytics pulls performance back in — which hooks worked, when to post, what your audience actually responds to — and feeds it all into memory.
